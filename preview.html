Methodology

A repeatable diagnostic, not a one-off snapshot.

We run repeated diagnostics because AI systems are probabilistic and change over time. The process is built to reveal patterns, not isolated outputs.

1

Define the buyer prompt universe

Build prompt sets around category, comparison, problem-aware, alternative, local, vertical, and objection-based buyer questions.

2

Run repeated measurements

Test selected prompts across target AI systems over a defined window to observe patterns rather than isolated outputs.

3

Extract mentions and citations

Capture whether you appear, which competitors appear, which domains are cited, and what the answer claims.

4

Classify source types

Group sources into brand-owned, earned media, review sites, directories, social/community, partner, competitor, or unknown.

5

Score visibility and influence

Separate basic presence from answer influence, so you know whether the brand merely appears or actually shapes the response.

6

Verify claims

Flag unsupported, outdated, contradicted, or incomplete AI statements for review and correction.

7

Translate findings into actions

Recommend content, source, authority, schema, messaging, review, or vendor-management actions tied to observed gaps.

Measurement

Metrics that connect AI visibility to decisions.

We measure and improve the signals that influence AI answer visibility — reported in terms executives can act on.

AI visibility score

Composite index of mentions, source presence, competitor comparison, and answer quality.

Mention share

Brand appearances divided by total tested opportunities.

Competitor displacement rate

Prompts where competitors appear and you do not.

Citation source quality

Quality and relevance of the sources shaping the answer.

Claim accuracy rate

Share of AI claims supported by approved or credible sources.

Action completion & re-audit movement

Actions completed during the sprint, and the change in visibility and accuracy after intervention.

A note on what is measurable: AI visibility is measurable and influenceable, not fully controllable. Results can vary by model, geography, account state, personalization, query wording, source availability, and time.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this SEO? +

It overlaps with SEO, but it is broader. SEO focuses on search performance. Signal Harbor focuses on AI answer visibility, source influence, and brand accuracy across AI-mediated research. SEO is expanding into AI visibility, answer engines, and citation ecosystems.

What platforms do you test? +

We currently run structured prompt testing across ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, and Grok. The exact set depends on your category, market, and where your buyers are likely to research.

Can you guarantee that AI tools recommend us? +

No, and no credible provider should. We measure and improve the signals that make accurate inclusion more likely. AI visibility is measurable and influenceable, not fully controllable.

Do we need to give admin access? +

No. Most pilots can start with documents, exports, public pages, and current reports. Read-only access can be added later if it is useful.

How is this different from a screenshot audit? +

A screenshot is a single moment in time. We use structured prompt sets, repeated measurements, source classification, claim review, and action planning — because AI systems are probabilistic and change over time.

Can you work with our existing agency? +

Yes. Signal Harbor can supplement internal teams, collaborate with agencies, or provide independent vendor accountability.

What happens after the diagnostic? +

You receive a prioritized roadmap and can choose advisory support, co-managed implementation, full-service optimization, or ongoing monitoring.

What does pricing depend on? +

Pricing depends on prompt volume, the number of engines tested, reporting depth, implementation scope, and whether ongoing monitoring is included. We scope it on a diagnostic call.

Who is this best for? +

Companies whose buyers run comparison-heavy research before contacting sales — B2B SaaS, professional services, compliance-heavy industries, multi-location service businesses, enterprise brands with agencies, and high-consideration consumer brands.
