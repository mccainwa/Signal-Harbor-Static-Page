**§3 Problem**
Title: **Search is moving from links to answers.**
Intro: For two decades, being found meant ranking on Google. Now buyers ask AI directly — and it replies with a short recommendation, not ten links. If you're not in that answer, you're not in the running.

- *The shift is here.* Customers ask AI "who's the best [service] near me?" and act on the reply.
- *Ranking ≠ being recommended.* You can top Google and never appear in an AI answer. Different systems, different rules.
- *AI can get you wrong.* It may push competitors, cite outdated pages, or state false details about you as fact.

**§4 Solution / outcomes**
Title: **Know exactly how AI sees your business — and fix it.**
Intro: Signal Harbor runs the prompts your customers would ask, records what each AI system says, and turns it into a score you can act on.

- Appear in more AI recommendations in your category and area.
- Get cited accurately instead of skipped or misquoted.
- See where competitors are winning the recommendation — and close the gap.

Rules baked in:

## 8. Visual Design System

**Color tokens**
- `--navy: #0A1628` — primary dark / authority sections, hero, footer.
- `--navy-deep: #060E1A` *(suggested)* — secondary panels, trust strip, card insets on dark.
- `--blue: #00C2FF` — accents, primary buttons, key data points. Use sparingly so it stays high-impact.
- `--blue-soft: rgba(0,194,255,.12)` *(suggested)* — glow/hover/active fills on dark.
- `--white: #FFFFFF`, `--off-white: #F5F7FA` *(suggested)* — light section backgrounds.
- `--gray: #6B7686` *(suggested)* — muted body/labels.
- `--alert: #FF5A5F` / `--warn: #FFB020` *(suggested)* — hallucination flags only. Reserve so the signal stays meaningful.

**Type**
- Headings: Sora-style. H1 oversized with tight tracking; clear step-down to H2 section titles, H3 card titles.
- Body: DM Sans-style, generous line-height, readable measure (~65–75 chars in light sections).
- Hierarchy from size/weight, not color.

**Backgrounds & rhythm**
- Alternate dark→light→dark. Premium navy-to-navy gradient acceptable in hero; avoid multi-hue gradients.
- Generous vertical spacing; consistent section padding.

**Surfaces**
- Cards on dark: `--navy-deep` with a 1px hairline border at low-opacity blue and subtle shadow. On light: white cards on off-white with soft shadow.
- One consistent corner radius (e.g., 12–16px). One thin-line icon set, one icon per framework card, nothing decorative.

**Buttons**
- Primary: solid `--blue` (navy text on light bg; or blue on navy). Secondary: ghost/outline. Clear hover (soft glow) + visible focus ring.

**Accessibility**
- Don't use `--blue` for small body text on navy (contrast fails) — reserve for large text, accents, buttons.
- Semantic headings (one H1), visible focus, touch-sized targets, hallucination flags carry icon + label, not color alone.

**Responsive**
- Multi-card rows → single column. Comparison table → stacked rows. Keep a CTA always reachable (sticky nav button or sticky bottom bar on mobile). Keep the score dial large on mobile.

## 10. What to Build First

**Principle:** ship the page that can book an audit first; layer proof and depth after; never block launch on assets you don't have — use labeled placeholders, go live, replace.
